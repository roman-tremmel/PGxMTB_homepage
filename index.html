About the project

Bringing pharmacogenomics to the molecular tumour board

Molecular tumour boards (MTBs) recommend targeted therapies based on the molecular profile of a patient's tumour. Yet how well a patient tolerates and responds to a drug also depends on inherited and tumour-specific variation in genes that govern drug absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion (ADME) and drug targets – the field of pharmacogenomics (PGx). This information is rarely considered in MTB decisions today.

PGxMTB is the first EU project to systematically integrate PGx into molecular tumour boards. Building on germline and tumour multiomics data from the nationwide precision oncology programmes DKFZ/NCT/DKTK MASTER and MAPPYACTS1/2, the consortium develops a PGx pipeline covering more than 300 pharmacogenes relevant for cancer and supportive care medicines, validates it in adult and paediatric cohorts, and explores liver-derived extracellular vesicles as a liquid-biopsy readout of hepatic drug metabolism.

Together with patient representatives as research partners, PGxMTB prepares the clinical implementation of PGx in MTBs at two leading cancer centres and evaluates its health-economic impact.

Objectives

What we aim to achieve

Predict PGx phenotypes

Combine germline and somatic variants with tumour multiomics data to predict drug response and toxicity, including the impact of rare variants.

Validate in independent cohorts

Confirm functional variants experimentally and validate PGx and drug-target signatures in adult and paediatric cancer patients.

Measure liver phenotypes

Use liver-derived extracellular vesicles as a minimally invasive liquid biopsy to capture hepatic drug metabolism in patients with cancer.

Implement in clinical practice

Establish a framework for PGx reporting in MTBs in Heidelberg and Paris, and train healthcare providers.

Involve patients

Engage patient representatives as research partners and develop materials that help patients understand PGx.

Evaluate the value

Assess the health-economic impact of implementing PGx in molecular tumour boards across European health systems.

Work packages

How the project is organised

WP1Lead: IKP

PGx pipeline & multiomics in MASTER

Refinement of a PGx pipeline for prediction of PGx phenotypes and drug targets in cancer patients, complemented by multiomics data in MASTER.

Tasks
  • T1.1 Germline PGx profiles including rare PGx variants for the selected PGxMTB drugs
  • T1.2 Somatic PGx profiles including rare PGx variants for the selected PGxMTB drugs
  • T1.3 PGx landscaping in multiomics data and identification of novel PGx signatures related to cancer drugs
WP2Lead: Karolinska Institutet

Functional impact of rare PGx variation

Assessment of the functional impact of rare pharmacogenetic variation.

Tasks
  • T2.1 Systematic in silico functional predictions of rare variants in pharmacogenes
  • T2.2 In vitro experimental validation of candidate functional variants underlying ADME phenotypes
WP3Lead: Gustave Roussy

Validation & paediatric extension

Validation of multiomics data of pharmacogenes from MASTER in RATIONALE and extension to a paediatric and adolescent population from MAPPYACTS1/2.

Tasks
  • T3.1 Validation of molecular PGx and drug-target signatures in independent adult cohorts (MASTER2, RATIONALE)
  • T3.2 Extension to a paediatric and adolescent population from MAPPYACTS1/2
WP4Lead: IKP

Hepatic PGx phenotypes via liver EVs

Elucidation of hepatic PGx phenotypes of cancer patients using liver-derived extracellular vesicles to assess the impact of tumours.

Tasks
  • T4.1 Isolation of liver EVs and characterisation of EV isolates
  • T4.2 PGx phenotyping of liver EVs using proteomics, metabolomics and epigenetics
WP5Lead: DKFZ / NCT Heidelberg

PGx implementation in MTBs & patient participation

Development of an implementation approach for PGx information in MTBs and involvement of patient participation.

Tasks
  • T5.1 Framework for PGx implementation in MTBs at NCT Heidelberg (MASTER) and Gustave Roussy (MAPPYACTS2)
  • T5.2 Enhancing patient engagement and training of healthcare providers for PGx
WP6Lead: UMIT TIROL

Health economic evaluation

Health economic (HE) evaluation of the PGx implementation in molecular tumour boards.
