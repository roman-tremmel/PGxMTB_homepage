items:
  - title: Predict PGx phenotypes
    icon: dna
    color: wp1
    text: >-
      Combine germline and somatic variants with tumour multiomics data to predict drug response and
      toxicity, including the impact of rare variants.
  - title: Validate in independent cohorts
    icon: flask
    color: wp2
    text: >-
      Confirm functional variants experimentally and validate PGx and drug-target signatures in adult and
      paediatric cancer patients.
  - title: Measure liver phenotypes
    icon: network
    color: wp4
    text: >-
      Use liver-derived extracellular vesicles as a minimally invasive liquid biopsy to capture hepatic drug
      metabolism in patients with cancer.
  - title: Implement in clinical practice
    icon: people
    color: wp5
    text: >-
      Establish a framework for PGx reporting in molecular tumour boards in Heidelberg and Paris, and train
      healthcare providers.
  - title: Involve patients
    icon: heart
    color: wp3
    text: >-
      Engage patient representatives as research partners and develop materials that help patients
      understand PGx.
  - title: Evaluate the value
    icon: chart
    color: wp6
    text: >-
      Assess the health-economic impact of implementing PGx in molecular tumour boards across European
      health systems.
