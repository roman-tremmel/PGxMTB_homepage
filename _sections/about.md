How well a patient tolerates and responds to a cancer drug depends on genes that govern drug absorption,
distribution, metabolism and excretion (ADME) and on drug targets – the field of pharmacogenomics (PGx).
This information is rarely considered in molecular tumour board decisions today.

Building on germline and tumour multiomics data from the precision oncology programmes
**DKFZ/NCT/DKTK MASTER** and **MAPPYACTS1/2**, the consortium develops a PGx pipeline covering more than
300 pharmacogenes, validates it in adult and paediatric cohorts, explores liver-derived extracellular
vesicles as a liquid biopsy of hepatic drug metabolism, and prepares clinical implementation together
with patient representatives.
