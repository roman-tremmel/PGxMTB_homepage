Thank you for visiting the website of the PGxMTB consortium. The protection of your personal data is important to us. This statement explains which data are processed when you use this website and for what purpose. This website is deliberately kept simple: it sets no cookies, uses no tracking or web analysis tools, embeds no social media plug-ins and loads no content from third-party servers. Fonts and images are delivered from the same server as the website itself.

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the website, your browser automatically transmits information that GitHub processes in server log files in order to deliver the website and to ensure its security and stability. These are in particular:

  • IP address of the device used to access the website
  • date and time of access
  • name of the page or file accessed and the amount of data transferred
  • website from which the page was accessed (referrer URL)
  • browser type and version, operating system
  • HTTP status code

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure and reliable provision of the website. We do not combine these data with other data sources and do not use them to identify visitors. As GitHub is based in the USA, personal data may be transferred to a third country. GitHub states that such transfers are safeguarded by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Further information is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cookies and web analysis

This website does not set cookies and does not use web analysis or tracking tools. No user profiles are created.

Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, e-mail address, content of your message) solely to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, or Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract. We delete the data once your enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention periods apply.

External links

This website contains links to external websites, for example those of the consortium partners, EP PerMed, PubMed and journal publishers. When you follow such a link, the operator of the target website may process your data (such as your IP address). We have no influence on this processing and assume no responsibility for it. Please refer to the privacy statements of the respective websites.

Security

This website is delivered exclusively via an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Our employees and service providers are obliged to maintain confidentiality and to comply with applicable data protection law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise them, please contact us using the details in the "Controller" section.

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR): you may request confirmation as to whether we process your personal data and, if so, access to these data.
  • Rectification and erasure (Art. 16, 17 GDPR): you may request the correction of inaccurate data and, where the statutory requirements are met, the deletion of your data.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): you may request that the processing of your data be restricted, where the statutory requirements are met.
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR): you may object at any time to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): you may receive data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format or have them transferred to another controller.
